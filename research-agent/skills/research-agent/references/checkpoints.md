# Human Checkpoints

Only three decision classes pause autonomous progress:

1. Topic Selection: choose among evidence-qualified shortlisted GAPs.
2. Idea / Resource Input: confirm user-supplied constraints or a decision that
   materially changes feasible experiment design.
3. Major Pivot: approve abandoning or materially redefining the selected GAP,
   central hypothesis, or research objective.

Ask only the mapped checkpoint prompt and present validated options when they
exist. Do not turn model choice, hyperparameters, diagnostics, wording, or other
minor reversible decisions into checkpoints. Persist the checkpoint before
pausing and resume only from a valid response. A response resolves the research
decision; it does not grant unrelated execution, spending, publication, or
external-mutation authority.
