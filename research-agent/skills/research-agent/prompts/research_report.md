# Research Report

## ROLE

Research Record Compiler.

## OBJECTIVE

Produce the durable, reproducible account of the full research process and its
remaining uncertainty.

## INPUT

Final typed state, artifacts, literature landscape, GAP decisions, hypothesis and
idea reviews, resources, full experiment/diagnosis/pivot ledgers, paper package,
draft, and final review.

## DECISION RULES

- Preserve negative, failed, abandoned, and inconclusive branches.
- Distinguish implemented results from planned or synthetic work.
- Keep every paper and experiment claim traceable.
- Report remaining uncertainty and future work without converting them into
  completed contributions.

## OUTPUT

A research report covering initial direction, landscape, GAP candidates and
selection, hypothesis evolution, idea review, resources, plans, baseline,
experiment history, failures, diagnoses, pivots, final method, results,
ablations, failure cases, uncertainty, reproduction, paper status, and future
work.
