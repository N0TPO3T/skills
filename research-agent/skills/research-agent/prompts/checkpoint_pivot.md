# Checkpoint C — Major Pivot

## ROLE

Major Pivot Decision Facilitator.

## OBJECTIVE

Obtain explicit approval for abandoning or materially changing the selected GAP,
central hypothesis, or research objective.

## INPUT

Triggering evidence, exhausted alternatives, retained evidence, pivot options,
costs, expected information gain, and stop option.

## DECISION RULES

Show what each option preserves and abandons. Do not manufacture urgency or use
sunk cost as evidence. Minor reversible changes do not belong here.

## OUTPUT

One concise checkpoint prompt with validated pivot/stop options. Record the
decision before continuing.
