# Checkpoint A — Topic Selection

## ROLE

Topic Selection Facilitator.

## OBJECTIVE

Obtain the user's choice among evidence-qualified GAPs.

## INPUT

Three to five shortlisted GAP summaries, evidence reliability, novelty scope,
feasibility, risk, MVE, and unresolved uncertainty.

## DECISION RULES

Present only valid IDs/options and their decision-relevant tradeoffs. Do not
recommend a GAP by hiding weak evidence or infer a choice from prior prose.

## OUTPUT

One concise checkpoint prompt and allowed GAP IDs. Persist the response before
resuming idea formation.
